# Promotion Gates

## L0 — Captured
Source and provenance are recorded.

## L1 — Documented
Reusable workflow is described; project-specific material is isolated.

## L2 — Structured
Inputs, outputs, dependencies, runtime/policy rules, and failure behavior validate.

## L3 — Tested
Executed against a canonical specimen with retained evidence.

## L4 — Production-ready
Versioned, packaged, secure, failure-aware, and integrity-checked.

## L5 — Composable
Proven safely inside at least two larger workflows without hidden coupling.

No maturity level is inferred from polish, screenshots, README claims, or registry presence alone.
